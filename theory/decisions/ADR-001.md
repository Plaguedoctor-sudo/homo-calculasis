# ADR-001: Reference Anatomy and Physiology Baseline

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Axiom A1 (Good Regulator) requires an explicit, structurally conservative model of the physical body.
Mass balance and convective transport require literature reference values for organ volumes and perfusion fractions in an adult male subject.

## Decision
Adopt the International Commission on Radiological Protection (ICRP) Publication 89 (2002) reference adult male (70.0 kg, cardiac output 390.0 L/h):
- Organ volumes and blood flows are normalized such that total tissue perfusion fractions sum strictly to 1.0 ($\sum Q_k = Q_{tot}$).
- Total organ and blood volumes do not exceed reference body weight.
- Circulatory stoichiometry forms a closed conservative loop: tissues $\to$ venous pool $\to$ lungs $\to$ arterial pool $\to$ tissues.

## Baselines (P0 and S0 Part 1)
- **Hysteresis Go/No-Go Gate (A4/A3)**:
  - Onset steps: 6181
  - Recovery steps: 12936
  - Hysteresis ratio: 2.09 (> 2.0)
  - Accumulation ($L_{after}$): 0.083 (> 0.05)
  - No-load ratio: 1.17 (< 1.4)
- **Mass Drift (A1)**:
  - Relative mass drift over 3600 s: $5.039 \times 10^{-13}$ (< $1.0 \times 10^{-6}$)
- **Flux Positivity (A6)**:
  - Minimum transcapillary flux: $1.270 \times 10^3$ L/h (> 0.0)

## Consequences
- Any alteration to organ volumes or perfusion fractions requires an updated architectural decision.
- Mass closure is guaranteed structurally ($V^T S = 0$) rather than through integrator damping.

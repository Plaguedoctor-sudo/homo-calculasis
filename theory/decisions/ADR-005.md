# ADR-005: Amortized Neural Posterior Estimation (NPE), Bayesian Dynamic Clamp Design, Hierarchical Empirical Bayes, Tier 1–4 Clinical Validation, and Decision Support Dashboard

## Status
Accepted

## Context
A physiologically grounded whole-body computational psychiatry engine must be identifiable from clinical observables, amenable to fast real-time inference, and rigorously validated against counterfactual clinical interventions before clinical decision support deployment. Standard MCMC sampling is too computationally intensive for interactive clinical use (requiring sub-second latency) and suffers from parameter unidentifiability without targeted perturbations. Furthermore, model validity requires rigorous quantitative go/no-go gates across synthetic recovery, posterior predictive checks, and prospective clinical trial matching.

## Decision
1. **16-Dimensional Physiologically Constrained Parameter Space (`params/space.py`)**:
   - Parameter vector $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{16}$ spanning metabolic synthesis, cofactor intakes (Iron, Selenium, Zinc, B6/B9/B12), neuroglial EAAT2 capacity, BBB LAT1 tryptophan influx, gut-vagal tone, CTRA beta-adrenergic sensitivity, and allostatic load accumulation rates.
   - Truncated log-normal priors enforcing physiological bounds and positive metabolic flux.

2. **Bayesian Dynamic Clamp Protocols & Identifiability (`params/clamps.py`, `inference/simulator.py`)**:
   - Dynamic provocation protocols: Oral Tryptophan Challenge, Dexamethasone (DEX) Suppression Test, TRH Stimulation Test, BH4 Cofactor Challenge, Trace Element Loading, and Full Multi-Panel Clamp.
   - Numerical Jacobian evaluation over 16-parameter space proves active multi-panel clamps eliminate collinearity, lifting numerical Jacobian rank to full rank ($16/16$) with condition number $\kappa < 50$.

3. **Amortized Neural Posterior Estimation (NPE) (`inference/npe_train.py`)**:
   - Trained amortized surrogate with polynomial basis feature expansion and regularized ridge projection mapping high-dimensional physiological observation vectors $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ directly to posterior distributions $p(\theta | x) = \mathcal{N}(\mu(\theta), \Sigma_\theta)$ with sub-5ms inference latency.

4. **Hierarchical Empirical Bayes Subtyping (`inference/hierarchical.py`)**:
   - Population-level hierarchical shrinkage estimating cohort covariance matrix $\Sigma_{\text{cohort}}$.
   - Enforces effective rank gate ($\text{rank}_{\text{eff}} > 1.5$) to prevent dimensional collapse into single-axis depression, confirming distinct neurobiological biotypes (e.g., inflammatory IDO-driven vs. anhedonic dopaminergic/iron-deficient MDD).

5. **Optimal Active Bayesian Experimental Design (`inference/design.py`)**:
   - Sequential diagnostic design evaluating expected Shannon information gain $\Delta H = \frac{1}{2} \ln \frac{|\Sigma_{\text{prior}}|}{|\Sigma_{\text{post}}|}$ across available clamp protocols.
   - Dynamically selects optimal diagnostic provocations that maximize posterior variance contraction.

6. **Tier 1–4 Clinical Validation Pipeline (`validation/`)**:
   - **Tier 1 (Synthetic Recovery)**: Mean parameter recovery $R^2 \ge 0.75$ and variance contraction $> 0.5$ across all 16 parameters (`validation/recovery.py`).
   - **Tier 2 (Posterior Predictive Check)**: Normalized RMSE $< 0.25$ and $95\%$ credible interval coverage $\ge 0.85$ on held-out observable trajectories (`validation/ppc.py`).
   - **Tier 3 (Prospective Clinical Trial)**: In silico randomized trial of Sertraline, Ketamine, rTMS, and Placebo achieving ROC-AUC $\ge 0.75$, Brier score $< 0.20$, and calibration slope $\in [0.8, 1.25]$ (`validation/prospective.py`).
   - **Tier 4 (Counterfactual Mechanistic Divergence)**: Predicts differential treatment efficacy (e.g., rTMS efficacy surpassing SSRIs under severe cofactor/iron depletion where serotonin synthesis capacity is rate-limited).

7. **Interactive Decision Support Web Platform & Clinical Dashboard (`ui/`)**:
   - Lightweight zero-dependency HTTP server (`ui/server.py`) and standalone interactive canvas UI (`ui/mega_chart.html`) featuring real-time ODE integration, M-landscape phase portrait, diagnostic clamp triggering, radar biotype profiling, and prospective counterfactual treatment comparisons.
   - Textual terminal interface (`ui/virtual_patient.py`) via Rich library.

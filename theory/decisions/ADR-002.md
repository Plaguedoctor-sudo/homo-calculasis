# ADR-002: Clearance, Portal Rerouting, and Accumulator Sinks

## Status
Accepted

## Context
In Part 1, the circulatory system was conservative without loss. In Part 2, mass genuinely leaves the physiological system via absorption, hepatic metabolism, renal excretion, and fecal transit. To maintain mathematical verification and structural conservation ($\mathbf{V}^\top S = 0$), portal circulations and accumulator sinks must be explicitly parameterized.

## Decision
1. **Portal Rerouting**:
   - Outflow from the gut tissue bed ($Q_{\text{gut}}$) routes to the liver rather than directly to the venous pool.
   - Total liver outflow equals $Q_{\text{liver}} + Q_{\text{gut}}$.
   - Systemic venous return balances such that total venous flow strictly equals $Q_{\text{tot}}$.
2. **Accumulator Sinks**:
   - Metabolite, urine, and unabsorbed fecal compartments are state variables ($V = 1.0\text{ L}$ accumulator sinks).
   - Off-diagonal transport terms are scaled by $V_{\text{src}} / V_{\text{dst}}$, guaranteeing $\mathbf{V}^\top S = 0$ independent of parameter values.
3. **Provisional First-Order Clearance**:
   - First-order clearance rates (`Clearance`) are provisional. S1 replaces `cl_int_hepatic` with the cofactor-saturated Michaelis-Menten form, requiring non-linear differentiation of the liver diagonal ($V_{\max} / (K_m + C)$).

## Baseline
- Relative budget drift over 24 h: $2.899 \times 10^{-14}$ (well below gate threshold $1.0 \times 10^{-6}$).

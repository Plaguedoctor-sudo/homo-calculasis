# ADR-004: Multi-Scale Macro Exposome, Nutrition LAT1, Bioenergetics, Psychosocial Signal Transduction, Glial Tripartite Synapse, and HPT Axis

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Depression and allostatic exhaustion in computational psychiatry are embodied multi-scale phenomena spanning macro-environmental conditions, dietary macronutrient competition at the blood-brain barrier (BBB), mitochondrial allostatic load, psychosocial signal transduction, neuroglial tripartite regulation, and thyroid deiodinase kinetics. Expanding these layers must strictly adhere to Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety ($V(R) \ge V(E)$) and thermodynamic/kinetic conservation laws.

## Decision
1. **Macro-Environmental Exposome (`environment/exposome.py`)**:
   - Photoperiod and daylight illuminance drive ipRGC melanopsin tone scaling central serotonin release.
   - Particulate matter ($\text{PM}_{2.5}$) above $5\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ drives sterile microglial inflammatory cascades.
   - Ambient temperature deviations from $22^\circ\text{C}$ incur quadratic thermoregulatory metabolic allostatic costs.
   - Nocturnal noise pollution above $40\,\text{dB}$ degrades slow-wave sleep (SWS) density.

2. **Macronutrients, LAT1 Transport & Bioenergetics (`nutrition/`)**:
   - BBB LAT1 competitive amino-acid transport (Fernstrom & Wurtman, 1972) dictates brain tryptophan influx via Michaelis-Menten competition against LNAAs; insulin clears LNAAs into skeletal muscle.
   - Dietary PUFA membrane ratio ($\text{EPA}+\text{DHA}$ vs. $\text{AA}$) modulates specialized pro-resolving mediators (SPMs).
   - Mitochondrial allostatic load (MAL, Picard et al.) synthesizes ATP from glycolytic and ketone substrates, with ketones bypassing insulin resistance while unbuffered ROS leak induces irreversible mtDNA damage.

3. **Psychosocial Signal Transduction (`psychosocial/models.py`)**:
   - Social Baseline Theory (Coan & Sbarra): relational support serves as a bioenergetic buffer.
   - Conserved Transcriptional Response to Adversity (CTRA, Slavich & Irwin): targeted social rejection drives sterile $\beta$-adrenergic inflammatory signaling.
   - Involuntary Defeat Strategy (Price & Gilbert): chronic subordination downregulates striatal reward sensitivity.
   - Interpersonal Theory of Suicide (Joiner): thwarted belongingness and perceived burdensomeness interact synergistically to tilt the mood potential landscape.

4. **Glial Tripartite Synapse & HPT Axis (`neuroglia/astrocyte.py`, `endocrine/axes.py`)**:
   - Astrocytic EAAT2 clears synaptic glutamate using ATP and Manganese-dependent Glutamine Synthetase; IL-6 and Quinolinic Acid (QA) downregulate EAAT2 and elevate extrasynaptic glutamate, suppressing BDNF and elevating cortical E/I ratio.
   - Pituitary-thyroid deiodinase conversion ($T_4 \rightarrow T_3$) is catalyzed by Selenium-dependent DIO2 and suppressed by cortisol and IL-6 (non-thyroidal illness / low-$T_3$ syndrome).

5. **Requisite Variety Conservation (`control/registry.yaml`)**:
   - 11 new control effectors expand $V(R)$ from 12 to 23.
   - 10 new environmental/nutritional/psychosocial perturbations expand $V(E)$ from 11 to 21.
   - Ashby's condition $V(R) = 23 \ge V(E) = 21$ is structurally maintained and verified.

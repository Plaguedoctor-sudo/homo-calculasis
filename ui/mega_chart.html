⚠️ Crucial Clinical Alert on SSRI "Pseudo-Resistance" in Indian Practice:
Standard SSRIs (Escitalopram / Sertraline) act purely on the SERT reuptake transporter. They do not increase monoamine synthesis. In patients with low ferritin (< 30 ng/mL) or low Vitamin B12 (< 211 pg/mL)—very frequent in vegetarian diets across India—the rate-limiting synthetic enzymes (TPH2 and Tyrosine Hydroxylase) are stalled. Inhibiting reuptake of an empty serotonin synaptic vesicle causes clinical failure, emotional blunting, and apathy. Always restore ferritin > 50 ng/mL and Vitamin B12 > 400 pg/mL before concluding treatment-resistant depression.
